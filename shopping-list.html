Local shopping data

Product names, quantities, manually entered prices, selected and bought marks, aisle order, currency labels, budget and the last undo snapshot are stored on this device. There is no account, application server or cloud synchronization. These details are not sent to our servers or included in advertising requests. System backup and device transfer are disabled. Corrupt saved files are preserved for recovery rather than silently discarded.

Sharing and deletion

Remaining shopping items are shared as text only when you press Share and select a destination in the Android chooser. The chosen application processes that text under its own policy. You can delete products and undo the last change. To permanently delete all shopping data, undo snapshots, recovery files and ad preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > Shopping List by Aisle > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. We do not have remote copies of your shopping data.

Advertising

Developed with the use of Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.5.0. Ad requests can transmit an IP address, approximate region inferred from IP, device information, technical identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostics to Yandex and its advertising partners for advertising, fraud prevention and service operation. Transmissions use encryption in transit. Product names, prices and list content are not included. Device location tracking and advertising SDK app analytics reports are disabled. The AD_ID permission is declared so the advertising SDK can access the Android advertising identifier where available, subject to your advertising preference and device settings. The SDK may also use other technical identifiers.

Before advertising initialization, you choose contextual ads or allow personalized advertising. Ads are requested in both cases; availability depends on the network and ad provider. Change your preference in More > Ad privacy. Read Yandex Privacy Policy and Yandex Mobile Ads SDK terms for their processing and retention. We do not control Yandex retention; contact Yandex about data it processes.

AppMetrica analytics

The app uses Yandex AppMetrica to measure installations, app launches, session duration and return visits. The SDK sends Yandex technical device, operating-system and app information, a technical analytics identifier and installation/session statistics. Network requests transmit an IP address, which may be used to infer an approximate region. Product names, prices, quantities, shopping lists, budgets and undo records are not included in analytics events. User profiles and custom events containing shopping content are not sent. Device location tracking, advertising-identifier collection, Java/native crash and ANR reports and automatic purchase analytics are disabled in AppMetrica. This does not disable the advertising SDK's separate processing described above.

These statistics operate independently of your advertising-personalization choice. Choosing contextual ads changes advertising settings and does not disable AppMetrica. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local shopping records and does not automatically delete statistics previously sent to Yandex. Analytics data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and handled under Yandex's privacy policy, including retention and deletion procedures. Contact Yandex through its policy or the developer at jonkryl@gmail.com about these records.

Audience and support

This shopping organizer is intended for adults and is not designed for children. It has no in-app purchases or payment services. Contact jonkryl@gmail.com about privacy or support.
